Symbol
The symbol was inspired by the Korean character ㅎ, the first vowel in the KTO’s Korean name.
It represents a person’s opened arms to welcome people. The small ball at the top symbolizes the KTO’s role of promoting the Korean culture worldwide, the middle line resembling open arms symbolizes the smile of the Korean people, and the bottom circle resembling the body represents perfect harmony and eternity. The vibrant colors represent the enthusiastic and aggressive spirit of the KTO.
